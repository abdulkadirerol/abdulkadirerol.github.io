I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Central Florida, working with [Prof. Cüneyt Gürcan Akçora](https://cakcora.github.io/). My research focuses on Large Language Models (LLMs), Topological Data Analysis (TDA), Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT), Continual Learning, Model Merging, and Machine Unlearning.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Georgia State University, where I worked with [Prof. Uğur Kursuncu](https://www.ugurkursuncu.com/) on LLMs and Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs). I earned my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Bilkent University and participated in an Erasmus+ exchange program at EPITA in Paris.

Research Experience
======
**Graduate Research / Teaching Assistant (GRA/GTA)**  
**University of Central Florida, Orlando, FL** | August 2025 – Present  
Advisor: [Prof. Cüneyt Gürcan Akçora](https://cakcora.github.io/)

- **Topological Data Analysis:** Applied persistent homology to analyze topological changes in LLM attention matrices and identify projections for selective freezing and updating.
- **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) & Continual Learning:** Developed selective freezing strategies for efficient fine-tuning and selective updating strategies to mitigate catastrophic forgetting across sequential tasks.
- **Model Merging & Machine Unlearning:** Exploring topology-guided model merging and modularity, with applications to machine unlearning and selective removal of learned capabilities.

**Graduate Research Assistant (GRA)**  
**Georgia State University, Atlanta, GA** | August 2023 – June 2025  
Advisor: [Prof. Uğur Kursuncu](https://www.ugurkursuncu.com/)

- **Toxicity Analysis & Red Teaming:** Explored vulnerabilities in LVLMs, including LLaVA, InstructBLIP, Fuyu, Qwen, DeepSeek, Gemini, GPT, and Grok, using persuasive prompting strategies informed by social theories; applied hard/soft prompts, prompt engineering, and fine-tuning.
- **LLMs in Mental Health:** Conducted comprehensive evaluations of Llama and GPT for mental health applications, focusing on sympathy, empathy, bias, toxicity, readability, and coherence.
